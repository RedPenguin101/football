Notes on a Football Management Game
-----------------------------------

The idea isn't to simulate football, it's to echo the feeling of being
a football fan. The heightened highs and lowered lows. Homegrown
heros, agonizing let-downs from high profile signings. Staying far
away from the minutia of tactical settings and training schedules. No
freaking work permits, jesus christ.

When Lolujo plays FM, the tactics are not interesting. The long term
storytelling is interesting. Climbing up the leagues with a talismanic
youngster matching every new challenge. At his best he creates
personalities for the players, creates a bond between them and the
viewer. But the game should be doing more to help with that.

To sum up in a sentence, the primary goal of the game is to create an
attachment between the manager and their players.

The top focuses of the game:

1. A simple match engine that generates memorable narratives. Nothing
   too obvious, but the last minutes of cup finals should be dramatic.
   No huge array of tactical options.
2. Transfers should be tense, poker like. Maybe an auction system.
   Create a real sunk-cost fallacy feeling in the player.
3. A player interaction / event system almost like Crusader Kings, where
   situations involving your players come up, and create braching b-stories
   which can continue over weeks and months.
