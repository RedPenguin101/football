Match engine
------------

Divide the pitch into 9 zones:

- front left right center
- half left right center
- back left right center

Players have positions, and their position (plus phase - below)
determine the likelihood they are in each zone.

Match is tick based, divided into 'phases'.

- attack
- transition
- defend

while one team is in an attacking phase another is in another.

With each tick, the team with the ball team attempts an 'action'

- pass to another within the zone (sucess, fail, interception)
- pass the ball into another zone (potential for interception)
- attempt a run into another zone (potential for tackle)
- hold up play (potential for tackle)
- take a shot (potential for miss, save, post)

Which of these actions is chosen depends on situation and player
skill. A player with low long-shots is less likely to attempt a long
shot.

For each of these actions, various factors influence the success or
failure.

- Whether or not the player is under pressure (opponent players in the
  same zone)
- The skill of the player and of the opponent(s) involved

All of this is generated by RNG, probably a d20 modified by skills,
and score thresholds for the various outcomes.
