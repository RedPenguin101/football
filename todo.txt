------------

- return to natural position: on a shot, all players return to their 'natural' positions
- start collecting match stats so as to start refining outcome likelihoods
- implement player stats and impact on outcomes
- player stats affect the choices they make
- UI for match engine
- refine success so multiple outcomes are possible
  - shots can be saved if on target, but can also hit the goal frame or go wide, depending on execution score
  - shots can be parried, which puts the ball to a random player in the box
  - passes can go out of play or be intercepted
  - dribbles can lead to a miscontrol or trip
  - dribbles can draw the foul
- game tick iterates through non-ball holding players and makes action.
- vary time advanced based on action
- Long distribution for goal keepers
- Actions for long passes over a zone
- tackles and cards
- record minute and scorer of goals
- dribble action should allow for dribble inside when on the advanced flanks
- set pieces: goal kicks, corners, throwins
- set pieces: kickoffs
- action: hold up the ball, allowing team mates to make advanced runs
- Players can have bad-game or good-game modifiers which impact their rolls
- Player condition ticks down based on action and affects outcomes and off-the-ball movement
